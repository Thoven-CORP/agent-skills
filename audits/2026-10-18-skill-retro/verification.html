Verification, from first principles

2026-10-19 · what we build, what checking it costs, what slipped anyway, and the protocol that follows. Measured on this Mac today. Sources: AUDIT.md sections 3 to 9, bin/verify in both repos, git log since Oct 3 across all branches.

The gate is tuned for defects we do not have. Lint and type errors almost never slipped; design-system misses, spec drift and wrong-surface proofs did. The cheap checks that catch those either never run inline or do not exist, while the expensive ones rerun other people's tests for 13 to 50 minutes.

5. The protocol

Verify the change you made, when you made it, with the smallest check that can fail it. No step runs other people's tests more than once.

While editing seconds, every change

1

oxlint and tsc, whole repo 17 s

2

The file's own test, red before, green after, on the behaviour the ticket names 2 s

3

Component or token touched: the design scan suite 9 s

4

Backend: rubocop on changed files, the mapped spec, the controller's request spec

Before DONE minutes, once per ticket. Check your own homework

5

Every behaviour in the diff has a line "decision Dn, frame Fn", quoting the parent

6

Real route at 1440 and 390 beside the frame. Shared component, tokens. Look at it

7

Callers: tsc green; direct importers' tests once, not transitive, not per hunk

8

Mutation only on a logic hunk in lib, a service or a model, against its one test

9

Build only when it can break: routes, config, server files; Gemfile, schema, controllers

10

Show it: receipt block, grid, red and green lines. Then the question

Before merge

CI on the integration PR is the full run. Nothing reruns on push. The reviewer applies steps 5 to 8 to the diff and reads the assertions; it does not rerun what the receipt covers.
