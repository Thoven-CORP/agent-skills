# Bounce rules

Stop intake and write the reason on the source report. Do not open a loop ticket.

- Missing any required field
- No evidence link
- Fewer than two steps
- More than one Found by, Repo, or Project
- Duplicate of an open ticket (link it instead)
- Forbidden area (migrations, recurring.yml, billing, credits, auth, privacy or legal wording, customer email)
- S1 that needs Andres (data loss, outage, child-safety). Page him. Do not loop it.
- A question or a feature request wearing a bug hat
- Production-only mutation required to reproduce, and no staging path exists

Two-repo reports are not a bounce. Split them.
