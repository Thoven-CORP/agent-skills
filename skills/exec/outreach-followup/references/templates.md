## What NOT to do

- Do not re-pitch Thoven (product description, traction stats, advisors, raise size are all in the thread above)
- Do not change the subject — reply-to-thread keeps "Re: [original]"
- Do not use "chat" — "connect" only (per memory rule)
- Do not use em-dashes that AI typically inserts in parallel structures — keep the existing "—" in "I know you're busy —" but don't add new ones
- Do not add P.S. lines, signoff variations, or website links
