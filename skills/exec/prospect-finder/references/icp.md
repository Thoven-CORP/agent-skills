# ICP Definition & Seed Queries

## Ideal Angel Profile

- **Who**: Founder/operator who built and exited (sold, PE acquisition, or IPO) a company
- **Sector overlap**: EdTech, marketplace/platform, music/arts education, K-12, tutoring
- **Check size**: $10K-$25K (sweet spot $20K)
- **Geography**: US-based
- **NOT**: VC fund GPs, institutional investors, people who only invest via SPVs/syndicates

## Why These People

They understand:
1. Two-sided marketplace dynamics (supply = teachers, demand = students/parents)
2. EdTech sales cycles and trust-building
3. The grind of going from 0→1 with fragmented supply
4. Why music education is underserved by tech

## Seed Queries

Rotate through these. Each targets a different ICP angle.

### Tier 1 — High-fit (run first)

```
1. "EdTech marketplace founder who exited and now angel invests, US-based"
2. "music education or music tech startup founder who sold their company, angel investor"
3. "two-sided marketplace operator who exited (Outschool, Wyzant, TakeLessons, Thumbtack adjacent) angel investor"
4. "K-12 education SaaS founder who sold company to private equity, now angel investor"
5. "online tutoring platform founder who exited, angel investor, United States"
```

### Tier 2 — Adjacent fit (run after Tier 1 exhausted)

```
6. "arts education technology founder investor advisor"
7. "edtech founder who raised seed round and later had acquisition, now investing in education startups"
8. "homeschool education platform founder angel investor United States"
9. "music learning app founder investor (Yousician, Fender Play, Simply Piano adjacent)"
10. "teacher marketplace or tutor matching platform founder who exited"
```

### Tier 3 — Broader net (run when Tier 1+2 saturated)

```
11. "consumer marketplace founder exited now angel investing in education"
12. "subscription education platform founder sold company angel investor"
13. "former CEO education company private equity exit now startup advisor investor"
14. "creative education startup founder (art, music, dance, theater) angel investor"
15. "B2C education marketplace founder who bootstrapped to exit"
```

## Gap-Filling Query Templates

When a specific Angle is underrepresented, generate from these templates:

- **Marketplace gap**: "two-sided [vertical] marketplace founder who exited and angel invests"
- **Education-LMS gap**: "[K-12/higher-ed] [LMS/assessment/curriculum] founder exited angel investor"
- **Music-Mission gap**: "music [education/technology/instrument] founder investor [US]"
- **Founder-Solidarity gap**: "bootstrapped [SaaS/consumer] founder who exited under $50M now angel investing"

## Custom Query Generation (when seed queries exhausted)

Seed queries above are a **starting template, not a hard limit**. When all have been run within 14 days, generate fresh queries using these strategies:

### Strategy 1: Name specific acquirers/companies
- "founder who sold EdTech company to Vista Equity or Thoma Bravo, now angel investor"
- "education company acquired by Pearson or McGraw Hill or Houghton Mifflin, founder now investing"
- "founder acquired by Chegg or Course Hero or Instructure, angel investor"

### Strategy 2: Role-adjacent searches
- "former VP at Outschool or Wyzant or Varsity Tutors who left and angel invests"
- "EdTech accelerator mentor with founder exit background, United States"
- "education startup advisor who previously exited a company, angel portfolio"

### Strategy 3: Vertical deepening by Angle
- **Music-Mission**: "music instrument retail chain founder exited", "audio technology founder sold to Spotify Apple Amazon", "music school franchise founder sold company"
- **Marketplace**: "services marketplace founder exited (Thumbtack Rover Care.com adjacent)", "gig economy platform founder who sold company angel"
- **Founder-Solidarity**: "solo founder who sold company to PE firm now investing in startups", "bootstrapped B2C founder exited $10M-$100M angel investor United States"

### Strategy 4: Company-name seeding
- Use companies from already-approved prospects' backgrounds as leads
- "[Known exited company] cofounder OR early employee angel investor"

### Anti-patterns (queries that historically return 0 qualified)
- Broad creative arts queries ("art music dance theater founder") → returns nonprofit educators, not investors
- Generic marketplace queries without exit qualifier → returns VC fund GPs
- Homeschool queries → returns current operators without exits
- Queries without "United States" or "US-based" → majority non-US results

## Proven Anti-Patterns (from 270+ searches on 2026-04-07)

### Queries that return PE firm employees instead of founders
- "founder sold to Vista Equity / Thoma Bravo / Alpine / Serent" → returns employees AT those firms
- "founder acquired by Renaissance Learning / PowerSchool / Follett" → returns current company staff
- **Fix**: Search for the ACQUIRED COMPANY name + "founder angel investor" instead (e.g., "Thinkful founder angel investor" not "sold to Chegg angel investor")

### Queries that return 0 qualified
- "creative education startup founder (art, music, dance, theater) angel investor" → nonprofit educators
- "homeschool education platform founder angel investor" → current operators, no exits
- "home services OR freelancer marketplace founder exited" → current operators, no exits
- "two-sided consumer marketplace founder exited angel invests US" → VC fund GPs
- "music school chain OR music academy founder sold" → current operators, no exits

### Best-performing query patterns (>3 qualified per 10 results)
- Company-name seeding: "founder whose company was acquired by [Chegg]" → 4 qualified
- Bootstrapped exit: "bootstrapped B2C founder sold company $10M-$100M angel investing" → 3 qualified
- Music tech specific: "audio technology music software founder sold company" → 2 qualified
- EdTech operator-to-angel: "edtech founder raised seed, later acquisition, now investing" → 3 qualified
