## 2026-10-22 — no em dashes, "connect" not "chat", one proposed slot for scheduling

> reflect · 2026-10-22 · ea-vault · broken-rule · applied · seen 5

**What happened.** The vault email guide's Writing Tips said "Use em dashes", although no em dashes has been the default since 2026-06-24. On 2026-08-05 an email offering Jonas Tempel a list of times went unanswered. On 2026-06-24 a Calendly link errored with no fallback.
**Why.** The skill tells every run to read the guide first, and the guide still taught the old rule and an availability-window example.
**What changed.** SKILL.md Step 4 has rules that win over any guide example: no em dashes; "connect", not "chat"; a scheduling reply proposes one specific open slot found with Google Calendar `suggest_time`, also when a booking link fails. In both the vault guide (`Agent Knowledge/Guides/email-language-guide.md`) and the repo copy, the em-dash tip became "No em dashes", the availability-window example is gone, and the partner template's Logistics step asks for one proposed time.

## 2026-10-22 — warm advisor and partner replies reference the last call and cut the recap

> reflect · 2026-10-22 · ea-vault · skill-gap · applied · seen 3

**What happened.** Warm replies needed many live edits on 2026-07-16 and 07-17, and twice on 07-18 for Acton Main.
**Why.** Drafts treated warm replies like cold ones: hard metrics, role titles, recaps of what the person built, location references, "one-on-one", and lines that could read as snark.
**What changed.** SKILL.md Step 4: warm replies open by referencing the last call or conversation and cut those elements.
