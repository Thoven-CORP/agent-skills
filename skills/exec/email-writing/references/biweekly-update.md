# Biweekly updates

Rules for any Thoven biweekly update email. They were learned on the 2026-06-22, 07-07, 07-23 and 07-27 cycles and lived only in retired Mem0 until 2026-10-22.

Andres, 2026-07-07: *"biweekly metrics are directional ballparks — don't over-litigate precision."*

## 1. Name which biweekly

There are three audiences: **investor**, **teacher** and **parent**. Ask which one it is before drafting if the request does not say. They differ in recipients, metrics and tone. Never merge two into one email.

## 2. Andres's own draft is the anchor

When Andres supplies a draft, it is the voice and fact boundary. Polish grammar and flow. Do not add facts, sections, numbers, names or claims that are not in it. When he supplies no draft, model the voice on the most recent sent biweekly of the same type, not on an old template.

## 3. Numbers

- Take user and teacher counts from the admin panel, then subtract the test-account buffer before you quote them. Test and internal accounts inflate the raw count.
- Metrics are directional ballparks. Round them ("about 180 teachers"). Don't spend the session reconciling a number to the unit.
- Any number not confirmed by Andres this session is marked `[VERIFY: …]` in the draft.

## 4. No invented momentum

Write only progress that happened. No "momentum is building", no "huge week", no projected wins framed as done, no pipeline stated as signed. If a section has nothing real to say, cut the section.

## 5. Recipients (investor biweekly)

- Build the BCC list from the Notion **Biweekly Hub** (`📮 Thoven Biweekly — Hub`) Recipients & Soft Advisors database. Skip addresses the Hub marks as bouncing.
- **Missing-recipient check:** before handing over the draft, compare the list against recent call notes and the Hub's "To confirm" rows for anyone promised a spot on the list. (2026-07-23: Rohan Mahimker was missing although his 06-23 call notes said to add him.)
- After Andres sends, add a Past Updates row in the Hub and update Last Contact for the recipients.

## 6. Audience overlap

Recipients overlap: investors, advisors and prospects read the same update. Name only participants whose involvement is closed and public. Do not name in-flight prospects, advisors, or soft commitments.
