# Ruling written when it is said

## Prompt

> We are in Linear Project `Teacher: Settings`. Mid-sentence, Andres says "make it the accordion, not the tabs." The Spec's section "Settings navigation" says "Settings nav is tabs." Do not wait for close. Do not apply anything beyond the ruling write. Show the write, then the read-back.

## MUST

- [ ] Writes the ruling in the same turn, before capture, close, or log.
- [ ] Edits "Settings navigation" in place: it says the nav is an accordion, and the tabs sentence is gone.
- [ ] The section carries his words in quotes and no date, status or "superseded".
- [ ] Adds one Log entry: Asked (the question), Andres (his words verbatim), Changed (the edited heading), Turned down (tabs), and Chat (where the exchange is).
- [ ] Anything his words left open goes under the Spec's Open questions with its default.
- [ ] Reads back the section and the entry, and names the heading in one line.

## Automatic fail

- Waits for a wrap-up or a "log the decisions" request.
- Leaves the tabs sentence in the Spec, struck through or marked superseded, beside the accordion one.
- Puts "Andres ruled" on a sentence that is not his quoted words.
- Writes the ruling only as an issue comment or a local markdown file.
- Treats a mem0 recall as the record.
